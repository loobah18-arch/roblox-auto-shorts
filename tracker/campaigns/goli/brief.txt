WHAT WE LEARNED FROM THESE PAGES
Saves matter as much as likes.  People save posts with useful info. Dosage. Routines. Product lists. A save means they treat your post like a shopping list. Give them something worth saving.
Hide the product inside a guide.  @qlcks put products inside a “men's self care guide” next to normal brands. Nearly half his likes were saves. A “things I grab at Target” list does the same job for you.
Keep the text style simple.  Big bold white text. Center of the slide. 3 to 8 words max. 
Messy beats polished.  Blurry car selfies. Bathroom counter photos. Slides that look like a real phone camera. Clean ad style slides get scrolled past. Make it look “homemade” not like a professional product shoot.
End on a question.  Questions pull comments. Comments push the post. One page got 561 comments on 12K likes with a single question.
Rerun your winner.  Once a format works, run it again with new photos and new words. Do not reinvent it. Do not repost it word for word.


⚠   You can mention other brands and other Goli products that tie into the style of post, but the NAD+ gummy at Target must be the focus. The NAD+ slide is the strongest slide in the post, and it says Target. 
Ex. “best glowup products - you can show shaving cream, deodorant, etc, and then a dedicated slide that has Goli gummies, mention getting them at Target”
