As I devoured the legendary Dragon Fruit, its dark energy coursed through my veins, transforming me into a humanoid dragon. I unleashed a torrent of fiery blasts, annihilating the rival pirate crew, but my power came at a terrible cost. My body began to mutate, scales bursting from my skin, wings sprouting from my back. I felt my mind slipping, consumed by an insatiable hunger for chaos. Suddenly, a gargantuan, robotic, laser-wielding Sea King emerged from the depths, its eyes fixed on me. The beast spoke in a voice that shattered the seas, announcing my impending doom, and then, everything went black, only to be replaced by an eerie, pulsing glow, as I found myself trapped inside a gigantic, robotic, Blox Fruit dispenser, hurtling towards a collision with a planet made entirely of Cheese.
