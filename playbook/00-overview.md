# 00 — Overview & How Payment Works

## The System

Whop Content Rewards pairs **campaigns** with **creators**. Each campaign sets:

- a **rate per 1,000 views** (e.g. $2/1K),
- a **total budget**,
- **supported platforms**,
- and **requirements**.

You create a permitted post from the campaign's approved assets, submit it, and get paid based on the campaign's approval and view rules. Revenue comes from **approved, eligible views** — not from any AI tool, account count, or speculative approach.

## Core Loop

**Find → Clip → Submit → Learn**

## The Five-Step Payment Chain

1. **Join a campaign** — read every rule, asset link, budget detail, and platform restriction.
2. **Use approved assets** — only clip what the campaign explicitly allows.
3. **Post publicly** — to a platform accepted by that campaign, in the required format.
4. **Submit the link** — provide the post URL plus any requested media/analytics evidence.
5. **Approval → payout** — paid under the campaign's view, budget, threshold, and cap terms.

> **Do not confuse the two income streams.** Whop campaign rewards are *separate* from TikTok / YouTube / Instagram platform monetization. One does not replace the other.
