## Niche Selection Guide

Ranked by "clip physics" — visual proof, clear emotion, frequent story turns, sufficient approved source footage.

**Strong starters:**
- **AI, Tech & Product Demos** — hooks like "This used to take 3 hours"; best for demos, reveals, before/after. Don't invent results.
- **Gaming & Streamer Moments** — natural stakes, reactions, wins/fails; best for clutch plays and discoveries. Use only provided footage.
- **Sports, Collectibles & IRL Action** — tension, reveals, transformations; campaign rights matter more than fame.

**Needs more skill:**
- **Podcasts & Personal Brands** — high source volume; your job is creating context, not just subtitling.
- **Physical Products & Transformations** — built-in before/after structure; don't make unsupported claims.

**Beginner caution:**
- **Regulated / high-risk topics** (finance, medical, gambling, politics) — extra constraints; only test with clear written rules.

### The Niche Rule

> Choose the campaign where you can identify **five different story moments in the first 15 minutes of footage.**

## The Six-Point Campaign Filter

Before joining any campaign, answer all six:

1. **Is it clipping?** Confirm the campaign accepts repurposed assets. Don't assume UGC and clipping are the same.
2. **What is left?** Check *remaining* budget, not headline budget.
3. **What must you earn first?** Find any minimum payout/views before content reaches review.
4. **Where can you post?** Note only the platforms the campaign allows.
5. **What is the cap?** A per-video maximum changes your best strategy.
6. **Can you make 5 stories?** Scan the content bank *before* joining.

### Red Flags (skip or clarify)

- Missing source assets
- Conflicting title/hashtag requirements
- Unclear remaining budget
- Vague clip rules
- No practical way to add useful context
