# 04 — How Payouts Work

## The Five-Stage Process

1. **Post goes live** — must be public and comply with platform/creative rules.
2. **You submit it** — add post URL plus content media file where requested.
3. **It is reviewed** — status shows `pending`, `approved`, `flagged`, or `rejected`.
4. **Views are valued** — eligible approved views calculated at campaign rate, subject to minimum threshold and cap.
5. **Funds are available** — approved earnings appear in your Whop account; withdrawal varies by setup.

## The Three Payout Traps

1. **Minimum payout** — a post may not reach review until it earns the campaign's minimum.
2. **Per-video maximum** — a clip stops earning after a cap.
3. **Campaign budget** — even a strong post still depends on available funds and acceptance.

## Monthly Target Calculator

Converts a dollar target into eligible-view requirements.

**Assumptions:** approved submissions, no cap, sufficient budget, all counted views qualify.

**Formula:** Monthly target ÷ (rate / 1K) = approved eligible views needed. Then divide by submitted posts for per-post average.

**Example:** $5,000 target at $1.50/1K →
- ≈ 3,333,333 approved eligible views needed
- ≈ 6,173 per post across 540 monthly posts
- ≈ 111,111 qualified views/day

> This is why volume × consistency matters more than a single viral clip.
